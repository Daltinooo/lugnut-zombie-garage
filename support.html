How do runs work?

Each run costs one battery charge. Charges refill over real time. Survive until the timer ends to keep your scrap. If your car is wrecked, you lose the scrap from that run.

What does Sell the Shop do?

It resets your cash, tools and crew, and pays Blueprints for the skill tree. You keep your cars, weapons, scrap and zones.

My progress did not sync between devices

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud on both devices and ICLOUD SAVE is ON in the game's Settings. Open the game on both and give it a minute. If the saves differ, the game asks which one to keep and backs up the other.

How do I start over?

Title screen > SETTINGS > RESET SAVE. This erases all progress, including the iCloud copy.

The game feels slow

Low Power Mode drops the menus to 30 fps (runs stay at 60). Turn it off in iPhone Settings > Battery for the smoothest picture.
