Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

Lugnut: Zombie Garage collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. We never receive your name, email, location or anything else about you.

What the game saves

Your progress (cash, upgrades, settings and so on) is saved in the game's own storage on your iPhone. A small random install ID is also kept on the device so two of your devices can tell their saves apart. It is never sent to us.

iCloud

If you leave ICLOUD SAVE on, your save is copied to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can follow you between your devices. It is stored and handled by Apple under your Apple ID. We cannot see it. Turn it off any time in Settings inside the game.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game posts your best survival times and prestige count to Apple's leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive any of it other than what leaderboards show every player.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes its local save. To remove the iCloud copy, use RESET SAVE in Settings before deleting, or manage app data in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > iCloud.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email coldstartstudios1@gmail.com.
